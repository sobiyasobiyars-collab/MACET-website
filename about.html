About Our Institution

Marthandam College of Engineering and Technology (MACET) was organized with the primary objective of providing quality technical education to bright students, especially from rural areas.

The institution provides a student-centered learning environment with modern teaching methods, motivated faculty members and state-of-the-art facilities.

MACET is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), affiliated to Anna University and ISO 9001:2015 certified.
